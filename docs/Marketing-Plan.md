# The Swap Shop - Marketing Plan

## Project

The Swap Shop is a campus marketplace where students can buy, sell, and swap items with other students.

---

## Target Audience

Our primary users are:

- University students
- Students living in dorms
- Students looking for affordable used items
- Students who want to sell items they no longer need
- Students interested in swapping items

---

## Problem

Students often have items they no longer need but have difficulty finding other students who may want them.

At the same time, students may need affordable textbooks, dorm supplies, furniture, electronics, clothing, and other items.

The Swap Shop provides one campus-focused place for students to connect.

---

## Marketing Goals

1. Introduce students to The Swap Shop.
2. Encourage students to create listings.
3. Encourage students to browse existing listings.
4. Increase student-to-student transactions.
5. Build awareness of the platform on campus.

---

## Marketing Strategies

### Social Media

Use platforms such as:

- Instagram
- TikTok
- Facebook

Example content:

- New marketplace listings
- Student seller highlights
- Before-and-after item stories
- How-to-use videos
- Promotional graphics

---

### Campus Promotion

Promote The Swap Shop through:

- Campus flyers
- Student organizations
- Residence halls
- Campus events
- Student newsletters
- Digital campus boards

---

### Student Organizations

Partner with student organizations to introduce the platform to their members.

Potential organizations include:

- Student government
- Residence life
- Student engagement organizations
- Academic clubs
- Campus sustainability organizations

---

## Promotional Message

### Main Message

"Buy. Sell. Swap."

### Supporting Message

"Give your items a second life and find what you need on campus."

---

## Launch Strategy

### Before Launch

- Create promotional graphics
- Introduce the project on social media
- Create sample listings
- Prepare campus promotional materials

### Launch

- Announce the platform
- Share instructions for creating listings
- Encourage students to post their first item
- Promote popular categories

### After Launch

- Share new listings
- Highlight successful transactions
- Gather user feedback
- Improve the platform based on student needs
